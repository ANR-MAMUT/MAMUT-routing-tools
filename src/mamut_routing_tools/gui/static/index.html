Sampling method
How customer locations are chosen on the road graph.
POI categories
Customers sit on real amenities of the ticked categories.
Parametric attach
Customers are sampled from road intersections, ignoring amenities.
Hybrid
Mixes both by the share below; missing POIs are filled parametrically.
Manual
You pick the exact POIs on the map. Each pick snaps to its nearest road intersection.
Depot position
Which road intersection becomes node 1, the depot.
Centered
Closest vertex to the mean of all vertices — short, balanced routes.
Random
A uniformly drawn vertex, so difficulty varies with the seed.
Excentered
Closest vertex to the south-west corner — long approach legs.

The pool radius is the exponential decay distance: cluster attraction halves at this distance.

Demand type
The distribution the per-customer demand is drawn from. Type 6 is spatially correlated (heavy customers cluster on one diagonal), type 7 mixes a few bulky customers into many small ones.
Route size band
The target number of customers per route. A value r is drawn uniformly from the band and sets the vehicle capacity, which in turn gives the fleet size (ceil(total demand / capacity)). It is a target, not a hard route count.
